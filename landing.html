How it works

1

Allow the camera

The video never leaves your computer. Hand tracking runs in the browser.

2

Raise your hands

MediaPipe finds 21 points on each hand. A One Euro filter keeps the controls steady without adding lag.

3

Play, and watch it

Every gesture shows up on screen and the visuals pulse with the bass, so you can see the music as well as hear it.
